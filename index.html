Nº 01 The problem

Bidets exist. A bidet worth having doesn't.

This was never about habits. It's about rooms.

Western bathrooms were drawn in an era when water meant a plumber and a permanent fixture. Your bathroom never made room — so the habit never made it over.

What's on offer proves the point: seat attachments that need installing, travel gadgets that live in a bag, or machines too ugly to leave out.

So we made a bidet that doesn't ask. It stands on its own, holds its own water, and earns its place in the open.

Nº 02 The ritual

Three steps. No tools.

01 Fill it

Lift the lid, fill from the top. At 10 litres, you're refilling once in a while — not every day.

02 Switch it on

One dial. The electric pump keeps the pressure consistent — first rinse to last.

03 Rinse

Lift the wand, rinse, set it back. The hose tucks away behind. That's the whole thing.

Nº 06 Good questions

Asked, answered.

How does it work without plumbing?

Fill it from the top, switch it on, and the electric pump pressurises the water. Lift the wand to rinse, then set it back. No pipes, no fixed connection.

Is it hygienic?

Food-grade materials throughout, and fresh water every time you fill it.

How do you clean it?

The lid lifts off so you can rinse the tank out whenever you like.

Is the water heated?

Not in this version — it runs at room temperature.

How is it powered?

A USB-C rechargeable battery, with weeks of use between charges.
